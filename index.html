What Viora measures

A Peruvian platform that manages alternate bearing in olive groves. We measure winter chill, fruit load and thinning with field nodes and offline sampling, so every season depends on data, not on each year's luck.

Viora reads the load of your grove, tracks the chill of every winter and tells you when to thin. Steadier harvests.

The context

Tacna grows 81% of Peru's olives, yet every season is decided blind: one year there's fruit to spare, the next there's not enough.

In 2024, growers in La Yarada reported losses of up to 90%. In September 2025, a record rebound. They aren't two different stories: they're the two faces of the same ON/OFF cycle.
