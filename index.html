We're Viora.

What Viora measures

A Peruvian platform that manages alternate bearing in olive groves. We measure winter chill, fruit load and thinning with field nodes and offline sampling, so every season depends on data, not on each year's luck.

Viora reads the load of your grove, tracks the chill of every winter and tells you when to thin. Steadier harvests.
